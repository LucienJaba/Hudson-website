Carpentry, decks & fences.

Deck repairs, replacements, handrails, fences, and trim work in Teton Valley and Jackson Hole.

Get a Free EstimateCall (208) 671-8686

Decks take a beating up here. Snow sits on them for months, and the sun dries them out the rest of the year. Boards rot, railings loosen, and fences lean.

Sometimes a few new boards and a fresh coat of stain is all a deck needs. Sometimes it’s time to tear it out and start over. We’ll tell you which, and do the work either way.

We also build handrails and fences, and do trim work inside and out. When the carpentry is done, we can stain or paint it too.

What we do

  • Deck restorations
  • Replacing rotted deck boards
  • Handrails and railings
  • Fences
  • Interior and exterior trim carpentry
