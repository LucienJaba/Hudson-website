House painting for homes and commercial buildings across Teton Valley and Jackson Hole, with the prep work that makes paint last through mountain winters.

Most paint failures up here come from prep, not paint. Sun at 6,000 feet and hard freeze-thaw cycles pull apart coatings that went on over dirty or failing surfaces. Every exterior job starts with washing, scraping, sanding, caulking and priming bare wood, so the finish coats have something solid to hold onto.

Inside, we protect floors and furniture, tape clean lines, and leave the house the way we found it, minus the old paint. We’ll also help you pick colors and walk you through paint options, so you know what you’re paying for and why.

What we do

  • Interior walls, ceilings, trim and cabinets
  • Log and cabin exteriors: wash, prime and repaint
  • Commercial and rental properties
  • Pressure washing and surface prep
  • Color and paint-product guidance
