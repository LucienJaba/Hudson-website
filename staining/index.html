Deck, log & timber staining.

Staining and wood finishing for decks, log homes, timber frames, siding and tongue-and-groove ceilings across Teton Valley and Jackson Hole.

Get a Free EstimateCall (208) 671-8686

Wood takes a beating up here. UV grays it, snow sits on it, and freeze-thaw opens up the grain. A good stain job starts with cleaning and brightening the wood so the new finish soaks in evenly instead of sitting on top and peeling.

We handle everything from a single deck to large timber homes, including interior wood ceilings, with clean taped edges and an even finish.

What we do

  • Deck cleaning, prep and staining
  • Log home and timber-frame staining
  • Wood siding and trim
  • Tongue-and-groove and wood ceilings
  • Railings, fences and outbuildings
  • Pressure washing and wood brightening

Serving Victor, Driggs, Tetonia and Felt in Teton Valley, Idaho, plus Jackson and Wilson, Wyoming.
